About

FOR MORE THAN A DECADE

Creating definitive designs and images in the competitive world of entertainment art as an award-winning concept artist and illustrator. His work has been featured in juried annuals Communication Arts and American Illustration, at the Society of Illustrators, and on the cover of Spectrum: The Best in Contemporary Fantastic Art.

Recently, his work was among the first-ever digital paintings acquired by the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art, and his design work on Apple TV's breakout series won a Concept Art Award from the CAA.
